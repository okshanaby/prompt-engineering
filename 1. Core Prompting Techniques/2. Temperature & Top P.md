### Temperature & Top P — Simple Summary

**Temperature** and **Top P** are settings used in AI applications to control how creative or predictable an AI's responses are.

### Temperature

* Controls the AI's **randomness and creativity**.
* Range: **0 to 2**.
* **Low temperature (e.g., 0–0.5):**

  * More predictable and consistent.
  * Chooses the most likely next word.
  * Good for factual or high-accuracy tasks (e.g., medical, legal).
* **High temperature (e.g., 1.0–1.5):**

  * More creative and varied.
  * May choose less likely words.
  * Good for storytelling, brainstorming, and creative writing.
* Very high values can make responses less coherent.

### Top P

* Controls **how many possible word choices the AI considers**.
* Uses a probability threshold between **0 and 1**.
* Example:

  * Blue = 80%
  * Gray = 15%
  * Orange = 5%
* If **Top P = 0.9 (90%)**, the AI only considers words within the top 90% probability range.
* Less likely options outside that range are ignored.

### Temperature vs. Top P

* **Temperature:** Changes how randomly the AI selects from available choices.
* **Top P:** Limits which choices are available in the first place.
* They can be used together to fine-tune AI behavior.

### Important Note

These settings are usually **not available in ChatGPT or Claude chat interfaces**, but they are commonly available when building AI applications through APIs.

### One-Sentence Summary

**Temperature controls how creative the AI is, while Top P controls how many possible word choices it can consider, allowing developers to balance creativity and reliability.**
