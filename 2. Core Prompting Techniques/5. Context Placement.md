### Context Placement — Explanation & Summary

**Context placement** refers to where important information appears inside a prompt or long conversation, and it can significantly affect how well a model uses that information.

Research (*“Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts”*) shows that even though modern LLMs can handle very large context windows, they do not treat all parts of the input equally.

### Key Findings

* Models perform best when important information is placed:

  * **At the beginning (best performance)**
  * **At the end (second best)**
* Performance is weakest when important information is placed in the **middle of long context**
* In some cases, middle-positioned information performs even worse than giving the model no context at all

### Why This Happens

Even with large context windows, models don’t perfectly “attend” to all parts of long inputs. As a result, important details in the middle can get effectively ignored or diluted.

### Practical Implications

To get better results when prompting or building AI applications:

* Put **critical information at the start**
* Reinforce important updates or constraints at the **end**
* Keep unnecessary or less important details in the **middle**
* Avoid overly long chats when possible
* Start a new chat when context becomes too large or messy

### One-Sentence Summary

**Context placement matters because AI models perform best when important information is at the beginning or end of a prompt, while details in the middle are more likely to be ignored or lost.**
